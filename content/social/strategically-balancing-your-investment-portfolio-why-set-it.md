## LinkedIn

A 60/40 portfolio can drift to 72/28 after three strong years in the market, without you making a single trade. Nobody decided to take on more risk. The market did.

For physicians building wealth mid-career, real estate professionals already concentrated in property, and retirees drawing income in Southeast Texas, that drift changes what "balanced" means for each of them. Fixing it by selling appreciated stock in a taxable account can trigger a capital gains bill you didn't need to pay. Rebalancing inside a 401(k) or IRA first, or directing new contributions toward the underweighted asset class, usually works better.
