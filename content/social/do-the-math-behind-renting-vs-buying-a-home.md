## LinkedIn

That $1,100 mortgage quote you got is not what homeownership costs. Add Jefferson County property taxes, Gulf Coast insurance, and a maintenance reserve, and a $220,000 home with 20% down runs closer to $1,700-$1,900 a month. Most buyers also need five to seven years to recover closing costs, so a two-year contract in Port Arthur usually favors renting before you even factor in what your down payment could earn invested elsewhere. The full breakdown, taxes, insurance, opportunity cost, and timeline, is in our latest post.
