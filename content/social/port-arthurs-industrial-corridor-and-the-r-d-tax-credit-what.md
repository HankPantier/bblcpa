## LinkedIn

A fabrication shop with $400,000 in qualifying wages tied to engineering and technical staff can generate a federal R&D credit running into five figures. That's the number buried in our latest post on Port Arthur's industrial corridor.

Fabricators, coatings crews, instrumentation contractors, and process contractors supporting Motiva, Valero, and TotalEnergies are doing R&D-qualifying work every week: new weld procedures for high-pressure lines, corrosion-resistant coatings for sour gas service, custom control logic for sensor integration. None of it requires a lab coat or a formal research department. It just requires meeting the IRS's four-part test, and most of these projects already do.

Texas businesses also get to choose between a franchise tax credit or a sales tax exemption on research equipment through the Texas Comptroller. Picking the right one can matter more than the federal credit itself.

If you run a shop supporting Jefferson County's refineries and plants, read the full breakdown: https://www.bblcpa.com/resources/port-arthurs-industrial-corridor-and-the-r-d-tax-credit-what
