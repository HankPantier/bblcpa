## LinkedIn

A private room in a Texas nursing facility can run over $8,000 a month, and Medicare typically won't pick up the tab for extended custodial care. Roughly 7 in 10 people turning 65 will need some form of long-term care, yet most retirement budgets never set aside a dedicated fund for it. Our latest post breaks down the three ways families actually cover this expense: long-term care insurance (best bought in your 50s and early 60s), the VA Aid and Attendance benefit for eligible veterans and spouses, and disciplined self-funding through savings. For physicians, retirees, and high net worth households across Southeast Texas, the sequence in which you tap taxable, tax-deferred, and Roth accounts to pay for care can shift your tax bill by thousands. Read the full breakdown, including the age-by-age planning window, before a crisis forces the decision: https://www.bblcpa.com/resources/long-term-care-how-you-can-financially-prepare-for-the-expen
