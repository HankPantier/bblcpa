## LinkedIn

Rent your Toledo Bend lake house or Galveston condo for 14 days or fewer this year, and none of that rental income touches your tax return. Cross into day 15, and the math changes fast: rental income becomes taxable, but expenses, utilities, repairs, even depreciation open up as deductions based on your personal-use ratio. On a $400,000 vacation condo, that depreciation alone can run about $10,900 a year.

Physicians, dentists, and retirees weighing a second property in Southeast Texas often don't realize the loan paperwork and the tax paperwork tell two different stories. Our latest post breaks down the 14-day rule, the personal-use test, and where depreciation recapture fits into the bigger picture: https://www.bblcpa.com/resources/how-to-turn-a-second-home-into-a-tax-win
