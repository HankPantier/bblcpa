## LinkedIn

A $22,000 medical bill can turn into a $13,000 tax deduction, but only if you've been logging expenses since the diagnosis, not waiting until April. An emergency fund covers the first bill. It does nothing to lower what you owe the IRS.

Health Savings Accounts offer something no other account does: pretax contributions, tax-free growth, and tax-free withdrawals for qualified expenses. For 2025, that's up to $4,300 for individuals and $8,550 for families, plus a $1,000 catch-up after 55. Physicians and dentists on high-deductible plans, and retirees managing Medicare costs, are leaving real money on the table by treating the HSA like a spare savings account instead of the sharpest tax tool available.
