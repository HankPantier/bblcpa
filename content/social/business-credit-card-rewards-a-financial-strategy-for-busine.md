## LinkedIn

Spend $50,000 on business expenses and earn $750 back? The IRS treats that as a rebate, not income. Your deduction drops slightly; you don't report a dime as taxable earnings. That's good news for Southeast Texas business owners racking up points on fuel, supplies, or travel.

The catch: sign-up bonuses earned without a spending requirement can be taxable, and nobody catches that distinction if the rewards account never gets reconciled against the books. A veterinary practice buying medical supplies needs a different card than a real estate professional putting mileage and marketing on a card, and carrying a balance at 22% APR to earn 2% cash back is a losing trade, full stop.

Our team builds rewards reconciliation into the monthly close so redemptions and bonuses land correctly at tax time instead of being guessed at every April.
