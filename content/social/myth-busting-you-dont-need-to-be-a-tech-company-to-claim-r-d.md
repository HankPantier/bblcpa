## LinkedIn

A shrimp processor testing a new preservation method. A restaurant perfecting a sauce recipe through three rounds of trial and error. A rice farmer trying a new irrigation timing. All three can qualify for the R&D tax credit, and none of them write code.

IRC Section 41 never restricted this credit to tech and pharma companies. The IRS runs any activity through a four-part test: permitted purpose, technological in nature, elimination of uncertainty, and process of experimentation. Restaurants, agriculture operations, manufacturers, dentists, and physicians across Southeast Texas clear that test more often than they think.

The catch: you can generally only amend the last three tax years to claim what you missed. Every year you assume you don't qualify is documentation you can't recreate later.
