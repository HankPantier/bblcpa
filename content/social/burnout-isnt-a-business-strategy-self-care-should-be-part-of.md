A Southeast Texas veterinarian missed two quarterly tax payments and overpaid a vendor twice, all in six months, because she was doing her own scheduling, payroll, and supply approvals without any backup. That's not a health story. That's a margin story.

Decision fatigue doesn't announce itself. It shows up as penalty interest, a missed coding error, a lease signed at 9 p.m. after a 14-hour day. Physicians, dentists, and veterinarians running their own practices carry this risk daily because the administrative load never stops, even when the clinical hours do.
