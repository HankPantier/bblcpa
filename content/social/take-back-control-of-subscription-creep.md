## LinkedIn

Fifteen dollars here. Forty dollars there. It adds up faster than you think.

A business running 10 to 15 software tools without a regular audit commonly finds several thousand dollars a year buried in unused seats, duplicate tools, and outdated pricing tiers. For a real estate office running three CRMs because every agent picked their own, or a veterinary clinic paying for both the old scheduling system and its replacement, that's real margin disappearing every month.

The fix isn't a one-time cleanup. It's a quarterly audit, one owner for the master list, and real-time visibility into your books instead of a surprise on next month's statement.

Read how to build that habit: https://www.bblcpa.com/resources/take-back-control-of-subscription-creep
