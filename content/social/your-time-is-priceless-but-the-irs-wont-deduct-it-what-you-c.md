## LinkedIn

Fourteen cents a mile. That's the IRS charitable mileage rate for 2024, and it's the deduction most volunteers never claim. If you drove meal deliveries, coached a Little League team, or served on a nonprofit board this year, your time isn't deductible, but your gas, supplies, and travel often are. A retiree driving 15 miles round trip twice a week to a Southeast Texas meal program racks up about $218 in deductible mileage. The catch: you have to itemize, and the 2024 standard deduction is $14,600 single or $29,200 married filing jointly. Physicians, dentists, and real estate professionals serving on hospital or trade association boards are especially likely to leave these deductions on the table because nobody kept the receipts.
