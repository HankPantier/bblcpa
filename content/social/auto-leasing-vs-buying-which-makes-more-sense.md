## LinkedIn

A truck over 6,000 pounds can qualify for a much larger first-year Section 179 deduction than a sedan. That single weight threshold changes the math on almost every lease-vs-buy decision we run for clients in Port Arthur and Beaumont.

Real estate agents putting 20,000 miles a year on a vehicle for showings usually come out ahead buying, since mileage caps on leases eat the savings fast. Physicians and dentists with steady, lower-mileage driving sometimes do better leasing and taking the straightforward payment deduction instead of managing a depreciation schedule.

Monthly payment is the number every dealership leads with. It's the least useful number for deciding anything once a vehicle touches your business return.
