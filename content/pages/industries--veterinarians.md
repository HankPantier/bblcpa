## Specialized accounting and CFO services for veterinary practices

Running a veterinary practice means juggling exam rooms, surgery schedules, vaccine inventory, and a staff that includes techs, receptionists, and associate vets. Add boarding, grooming, retail, and wellness plans as separate revenue streams, and the bookkeeping gets complicated fast.

Brammer, Begnaud, & Lattimore has served Southeast Texas businesses since 1950, and we've built our accounting approach around how a veterinary practice actually runs: seasonal cash flow swings, controlled substance recordkeeping, and payroll that doesn't wait for a slow week. Your books get built for animal hospitals, not stretched from a generic small-business template.

You went into veterinary medicine to treat animals, not reconcile bank statements. Let us handle the numbers so you can stay focused on your next patient.

## Year-round tax planning and support

Tax season shouldn't be the only time you think about taxes. Veterinary practices face specific decisions all year: how to structure a new X-ray machine or ultrasound unit for Section 179 deductions, how quarterly estimated payments track against a practice with a slow August and a packed December, and how bonus depreciation applies to surgical equipment.

We meet with veterinary clients throughout the year, not just in March and April, so these decisions get planned before they happen instead of scrambled after the fact. If the IRS sends a notice, our [audit protection](/services/outsourced-accounting/audit-protection) service handles the correspondence and represents your practice directly.

Year-round attention means fewer surprises each April and more strategy behind every equipment purchase, hire, or expansion your practice is considering.
