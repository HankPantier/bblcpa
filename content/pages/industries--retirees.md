## Tax and Accounting Services Designed for Retirees

Retirement should feel like the reward it is, not a second job spent untangling 1099s, RMD notices, and Social Security worksheets. Brammer, Begnaud, & Lattimore has guided Southeast Texas families through this exact transition since 1950, translating IRS rules on retirement income into decisions you can actually act on.

Our approach starts with a simple premise: your financial life doesn't get simpler once the paychecks stop, so your CPA relationship shouldn't either. We build a fixed monthly plan around your Social Security timing, pension income, and investment distributions, then stay in your corner all year, not just during the six weeks before April 15.

Whether you're newly retired from a Beaumont refinery job or drawing a pension after decades teaching in a Port Arthur classroom, the tax code treats every dollar differently depending on where it came from. We sort through that for one predictable price each month.

## Year-Round Support for Social Security, Distributions, and Investment Income

Tax planning for retirees doesn't end on April 15, because retirement income rarely arrives in one lump sum. Between Social Security, required minimum distributions, pension checks, and capital gains from your brokerage account, a decision made in July can change what you owe next spring.

Here's what we track for you, all year:

- Social Security taxation: We calculate how much of your benefit is taxable based on your combined income and flag strategies to keep more of it tax-free.
- Required minimum distributions: Missing an RMD deadline triggers a 25% IRS penalty, so we track amounts and dates before they become a problem.
- Pension and annuity income: Multiple income sources often mean under-withholding; we coordinate them so you're not stuck owing a penalty at filing time.
- Investment and capital gains income: Sales and distributions get timed against your tax bracket, not just your account statements.

This isn't a once-a-year conversation. It's ongoing [personal income tax](/services/personal-income-tax) planning that adjusts as tax law, interest rates, and your own spending needs change.

**Q: Do I still need a CPA after I retire?**
A: Yes, often more than before. Retirement income comes from multiple sources, Social Security, pensions, RMDs, and investments, each taxed differently. A CPA coordinates these so you avoid penalties, minimize taxes, and don't get surprised by an unexpected bill in April.

**Q: How much of my Social Security benefit is taxable?**
A: It depends on your combined income from all sources, including pensions and investment withdrawals. Up to 85% of your benefit can be taxable. We calculate your specific number and look for legal ways to lower it based on your full financial picture.

**Q: What happens if I miss my required minimum distribution?**
A: The IRS charges a 25% penalty on the amount you should have withdrawn but didn't, on top of the tax you already owe. We track RMD deadlines and amounts for every client so this penalty never becomes an issue.

**Q: Does Brammer, Begnaud, & Lattimore charge by the hour for retirement planning?**
A: No. Retiree clients pay one fixed monthly fee covering tax planning, preparation, and year-round questions about Social Security, distributions, and investment income. The price is set upfront and doesn't change based on how often you reach out.

**Q: Can you help decide if a Roth conversion makes sense?**
A: Yes. We run the numbers against your current tax bracket, future RMD requirements, and estate goals to show whether converting now saves money long-term or simply moves the tax bill to a worse time. You make the final call with complete information.
