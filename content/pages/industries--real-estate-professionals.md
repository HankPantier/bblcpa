## Accounting & Tax Services for Real Estate Professionals

Commission checks show up on their own schedule. Closing costs, marketing spend, mileage, and 1099 income don't pause just because the market slowed down for a month. Brammer, Begnaud, & Lattimore has worked with real estate agents, brokers, and investors across Southeast Texas since 1950, and we know the difference between a solo agent with three listings and a broker splitting commissions across a ten-person team.

Our bookkeeping, tax, and advisory teams build your books around how real estate income actually moves: irregular deposits, deductible marketing costs, and property-level reporting for anyone holding rentals or flips. That means fewer surprises at tax time and more hours back in your week for showings, closings, and client calls. Whether you're licensed under a brokerage or running your own shop, we handle the numbers so you can handle the deals.

## Year-Round Tax Planning & Deduction Strategies

Waiting until March to think about taxes costs real estate professionals real money. Commission-based income, vehicle mileage, home office use, marketing spend, and continuing education all carry deduction opportunities that get missed when tax planning only happens once a year. We build a plan around your actual schedule, not a generic template, and check in on it throughout the year, not just at filing time.

For investors and agents holding rental property, that includes depreciation strategy, cost segregation analysis, and guidance on 1031 exchanges so you can defer gains and reinvest instead of handing a chunk to the IRS. We also help set quarterly estimated payments so a strong closing quarter doesn't turn into a tax bill you didn't budget for. Our [business tax planning](/services/outsourced-accounting/tax) service covers entity-level strategy alongside your personal return, so nothing falls through the cracks between the two.

Agents and brokers make fast decisions. Deciding whether to take on another listing agent, invest in a marketing push, or buy another rental shouldn't wait on a bookkeeper's schedule.
